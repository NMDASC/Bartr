How we price it

A Bayesian ensemble, not one formula

Every piece of evidence becomes its own estimate of value, with its own uncertainty. Precision weighted. Wider when they disagree.

$$\ln V \mid k \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_k,\ \sigma_k^2), \qquad k \in \{\text{listing},\ \text{income},\ \text{proxy},\ \text{model},\ \text{base rate}\}, \qquad w_k = \sigma_k^{-2}$$
$$\mu = \frac{\sum_k w_k \mu_k}{\sum_k w_k}, \qquad \sigma^2 = \frac{1}{\sum_k w_k} + \frac{\sum_k w_k (\mu_k - \mu)^2}{\sum_k w_k}, \qquad V_q = \exp\!\big(\mu + \sigma\,\Phi^{-1}(q)\big), \qquad \text{price} = V_{0.50}/N$$

How the market works

Frequent batch auction with owner liquidity

The owner is the other side. The platform never trades.

Ask ladder. 3,000 shares offered at five prices, the P55 to P80 quantiles of the valuation. Buy while float remains.

Buyback floor. A standing bid for 1,000 shares at P20. You can always exit.

One price per round. Every 10 seconds the book clears at the price that maximizes traded volume. Everyone gets the same price. No market maker, no house inventory.

$$p^\star = \arg\max_{p} \min\{D(p),\ S(p)\}, \qquad D(p) = \sum_{\ell_b \ge p} q_b, \quad S(p) = \sum_{\ell_s \le p} q_s$$

Anti arbitrage

Enforced in the engine, covered by tests

Fair by construction.

No continuous matching, so speed buys nothing. The owner requotes only between rounds, never bids above its floor, never asks below P55, so the account that holds the float cannot chase the price. Server timestamps only; orders are immutable, cancels are new events.

Portfolio

Built for you to make money.

Edge on every suggestion. Model value against market price, shown in the open. We publish what we think it is worth.

Your risk, your dial. A Kelly multiplier from 0.25 to 1.0 sizes every stake to your appetite. Default is half Kelly, because the edge is an estimate.

Always an exit. The owner's floor is a standing bid. We are never your counterparty.

$$\mu = \ln\frac{v}{p}, \qquad f = \mathrm{clip}\!\big(m \cdot \tfrac{\mu}{\sigma^2},\ 0,\ 0.20\big), \qquad \$ = f \cdot B$$

\(v\) model value, \(p\) price, \(\sigma\) uncertainty, \(m\) your multiplier, \(B\) bankroll
