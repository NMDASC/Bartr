**Project Description:**
Bartr is the only platform that connects small businesses to investors, potential acquirers, and people who just wanna support local mom and pop shops. Our proprietary search algorithms and quantitative strategies allow you to seamlessly discover, trade and acquire small businesses: we rigorously rank and mathematically score companies for acquirability, suggest optimal prices based on industry-tested trading mathematics, construct portfolios, and host safe, arbitrage-free bidding rounds. We ensure that all of this is done securely through swarms of Grok agents reviewing transactions, flagging suspicious actors, and generating red-teams.
